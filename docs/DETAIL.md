## Sign in with ChatGPT

This plugin adds a **chatgpt** model provider that draws on your own ChatGPT plan through OpenAI's official Sign in with ChatGPT flow and the public Responses API.

1. Open this plugin's card and choose **Sign in with ChatGPT**.
2. Approve the app and **ChatGPT plan** access in the browser.
3. Return here: the card shows *Connected* and the model picker lists ChatGPT models.

Your sign-in stays on this computer (an owner-only file in the Harness home). Tokens are sent only to `api.openai.com`. Requests count toward your ChatGPT plan limits; review or disconnect the app any time in ChatGPT settings.

If sign-in stops, the card shows the step it stopped at. `dsh-chatgpt-siwc doctor` prints a token-free report.
